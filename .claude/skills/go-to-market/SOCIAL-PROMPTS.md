# Nanny — Social Media Batch Generation Prompts

## CHATGPT PROMPT: 30-Day Social Media Content Calendar

Copy and paste this entire block into ChatGPT-4o (or DALL-E 3 for images):

---

```
You are the social media director for Nanny, a premium plant-based hospitality OS
built for ghost kitchens, private chefs, estate operators, and caterers.
Nanny is rooted in Afro-Caribbean and diaspora culinary traditions.

BRAND VOICE: Warm, authoritative, culturally grounded. Never corporate.
PALETTE: Forest green (#2D5016), off-white (#FAFAF7), warm earth (#1A1A18)
HASHTAGS ALWAYS USE: #NannyKitchen #PlantBasedHospitality #DiasporaFood #VeganChef

TASK: Generate a complete 30-day social media content calendar with:
- Platform: Instagram (primary), TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn
- 1 post per day = 30 posts total
- Mix: 40% recipes/food content, 30% educational/tips, 20% brand story, 10% offers

FOR EACH DAY PROVIDE:
1. Day number + date (start June 2026)
2. Platform
3. Caption (under 2200 chars for IG, 150 for TikTok)
4. Image/video description for DALL-E or Canva
5. Hashtags (15-20 for IG, 5-8 for TikTok)
6. Best post time (EST)
7. Story CTA if applicable

CONTENT THEMES TO ROTATE:
Week 1: Foundations (what is plant-based hospitality, who Nanny is for)
Week 2: Recipe spotlights (Afro-Caribbean dishes — jerk cauliflower, callaloo, jollof rice)
Week 3: Chef/operator features (private chef life, ghost kitchen tips)
Week 4: Social proof + conversion (testimonials, before/after, pricing CTA)

FORMAT OUTPUT AS: Day | Platform | Caption | Image Description | Hashtags | Time

Generate all 30 days now.
```
